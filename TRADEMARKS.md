## The files

The logo files in [`web/public/brand/`](web/public/brand/) as well as `web/public/favicon.ico` and `web/public/apple-touch-icon.png` are released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>). You may copy, change and pass them on without asking.

CC0 only covers the copyright in the files. It grants no rights in the name and the marks as trademarks (section 4a of CC0). This policy covers those.

## What you may do with the name and the logos

- Name and show Enterprise Wiki: in articles, talks, trainings, comparisons, guides.
- In your own wiki running Enterprise Wiki, use the logos the way the wiki shows them (for example “Powered by Enterprise Wiki” in the footer).
- Offer plugins, services or trainings for Enterprise Wiki and say what they are for, for example “plugin for Enterprise Wiki”.

## What you may not do

- Give the impression that an offer comes from the project or is checked or recommended by it when that is not true.
- Use the name or the logos for a product of your own or for a changed version. Whoever passes on a changed version of Enterprise Wiki gives the result its own name and its own logos.
- Show the seal without a valid license installed. The wiki itself shows it only then.
- Change the name or the logos and use them so that they can be mistaken for the real ones.
